COMMANDS:

1.nmap:
●to perform simple ping
nmap -sn vulnweb.com
●to perform SYN scan
nmap -sS vulnweb.com
●to identify the service and version
nmap -sV vulnweb.com
●to scan a specific port
nmap -p 80 --script http-headers vulnweb.com
●to list host in network
nmap -sL vulnweb.com
●to perform UDP scan
nmap -sU vulnweb.com
●to identify the OS in network
nmap -O vulnweb.com
●to perform aggressive scan
nmap -A vulnweb.com
●to save output as file
nmap -oN outputfile.nmap vulnweb.com
